The parser sends prose, mixed text, and empty text to the original body path. Normal type uses two columns; accessibility sizes use one. Title, date, and card values have no fixed height.
- The Watch main cardio page retains elapsed time, machine level or distance/pace, heart rate, and calories. It adds a numeric zone label and the existing personalized `HRZoneBar`; unavailable heart rate remains `-- bpm` and an unavailable zone remains `Zone --`. Moving calories into the header makes its value smaller than before, but it remains present with its unit and a VoiceOver label/value.
- Final Watch header fit, first viewport visibility, actual contrast in all themes, large text wrapping, and VoiceOver reading order require final native validation. The focused UI tests contain assertions for default iPhone body composition viewport and Watch heart rate, zone, bar, and calories frames. The Watch test does not assert the level frame; verify that it remains fully visible in the final C02 capture.

The two values remain visible on their own stacked cards at maximum text size when scrolled. Generic text also remains readable in normal and maximum text sizes. The two generic maximum captures appear identical; they do not demonstrate a distinct third state.
